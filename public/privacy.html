Privacy & terms

Street Football is an independent, free, open-source fixture and highlights companion. It is not affiliated with the Premier League, clubs or YouTube. Updated 29 October 2026.

Your preferences stay here

Your favorite teams and time zone are stored in your browser’s local storage. Score reveals last only for the current page session. There are no accounts, advertising or analytics added by this project. Clear this site’s browser data to remove saved preferences.

Hosting and external content

GitHub Pages serves the website and may process request information such as your IP address under GitHub’s privacy statement. Opening a highlight preview loads thumbnails from YouTube’s servers; opening a video takes you to YouTube. These requests are covered by Google’s Privacy Policy. No embedded player or YouTube sign-in is used.

YouTube and football data

This site uses YouTube API Services. By using its YouTube features, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. You can review your Google account permissions; this site does not request access to your Google account. Video titles and thumbnails are supplied by YouTube and may reveal scores. Fixture matching is provided by Street Football. Videos remain on their original channels and may be unavailable in some regions.

Football data provided by the Football-Data.org API. Kickoff times, results and availability can change; this site is not a real-time scoring service. Public YouTube metadata is refreshed or removed within 30 days during operation. The source-code MIT licence does not license third-party data, branding or videos.
